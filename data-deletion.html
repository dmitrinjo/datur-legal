Data Deletion Request

Datur · Last updated: 16 June 2026

Datur is built offline-first: by default, all your data lives only on your device, and you can delete it instantly at any time. This page explains exactly how to delete your data, what gets deleted, and what happens if you've enabled optional cloud sync.

1. Delete your data directly in the app (fastest)

No account or sign-in is needed for this — it works for every Datur user:

Step 1. Open Datur and go to Settings.
Step 2. Tap Account.
Step 3. Tap Delete all data.
Step 4. Confirm the action.

This immediately and permanently erases all local data described in Section 3 from your device. If you had cloud sync enabled, it also queues your synced data for deletion from our backend (see Section 4).

2. Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or prefer to request deletion directly, email us:

To: dmitrinjo@gmail.com
Subject: Datur Data Deletion Request
Please include: the email address you used for sync (if any), and confirmation that you want all associated data deleted.

We process email requests within 7 business days and confirm by email once complete.

3. Data deleted from your device

Tapping "Delete all data" removes the following, stored locally by Android Room on your device:

  • App launch and interception events (package names, timestamps, outcomes such as abandoned/proceeded/locked)
  • Intent/trigger selections (e.g. Bored, Habit, Stressed, Escaping)
  • Focus Session records (start/end times)
  • App preferences (monitored apps, mode settings, delay durations)
  • Any display name you entered

This data is deleted immediately and irreversibly — there is no recovery period for local deletion.

4. Data deleted from our servers (only if Cloud Sync was enabled)

Datur does not require an account. If you explicitly enabled Cloud Sync in Settings, a copy of the data in Section 3 was backed up to our backend (Supabase) under an anonymous identifier — never your name or email.

  • Deletion requests (via app or email) remove this backup within 30 days.
  • If you uninstall the app without requesting deletion, backups are automatically purged after 90 days of inactivity.

5. What we cannot delete on your behalf

  • Google Play purchase/billing history — held by Google. Manage or request deletion via your Google Play account.
  • RevenueCat subscription metadata — our billing processor. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for their retention practices.

6. Questions

Contact Dmitrijs Drozds, developer of Datur, at dmitrinjo@gmail.com for any privacy or deletion questions.

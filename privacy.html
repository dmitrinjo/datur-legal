Privacy Policy

Datur · Last updated: 10 June 2026

Datur is a behavioral interception app that adds friction before you open compulsive apps. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: Datur is built offline-first and reads as little as physically possible.

1. What Datur observes

To intercept an app launch, Datur's Accessibility Service detects which app you are about to open. It observes only the package name of the foreground app (for example, com.instagram.android).

  • Datur does not read screen content — content retrieval is disabled at the system level.
  • Datur does not read your messages, posts, keystrokes, passwords, or any in-app activity.
  • Datur does not record your screen and does not take screenshots.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally on your device and is the source of truth:

  • Your settings (delay durations, which apps you chose to monitor, modes).
  • Interception events — a timestamp, the package name involved, and the outcome (for example: abandoned, proceeded, locked). This powers your statistics.
  • An optional display name you enter yourself.

3. Optional cloud sync

If you sign in and enable sync, the data described in Section 2 may be backed up to our cloud provider (Supabase) so it can be restored or shown across sessions. This is used solely to provide the service to you. If you do not sign in, your data stays on your device.

4. Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. Datur never sees or stores your payment card details. We receive only the subscription status needed to unlock paid features.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the infrastructure providers (Google Play, Supabase) strictly needed to run the service.

6. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Accessibility Service — to detect app launches and trigger interception. Package names only.
  • Display over other apps — to show the full-screen delay/lock overlay.
  • Usage access — to read app usage for statistics.
  • Foreground service — to keep the interception engine running reliably.

7. Your choices

You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time in Android settings, which stops all interception and observation. You can request deletion of any synced data by contacting us at the address below.

8. Children

Datur is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions or data requests: support.datur@gmail.com
